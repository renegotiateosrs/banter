# Banter

A RuneLite plugin that takes the piss out of you.

A tramp follows you around everywhere you go and delivers the roasts in person,
complete with an emote: a laugh when you die, a slow clap when you level up, a
yawn when you go AFK. He has nothing. He is still doing better than you.

Your heckler (default: *Dave the Tramp*) will chime in when you:

- **Die** – "Gravestone's getting a loyalty card at this point."
- **Level up** – "43 Woodcutting! Only 12,034,431 xp to 99. See you in 2031."
- **Get a 99** – "99 Cooking. Go outside. Please. The sun misses you."
- **Burn food** – "Gordon Ramsay just felt a disturbance."
- **Fail a pickpocket** – "Caught red-handed by a man with zero combat stats."
- **Drop to low HP** – "You might want to eat something. Just a thought."
- **Go AFK** – "Your character is just standing there. Menacingly."
- **Enter the Wilderness** – "Brave. Stupid, but brave."
- **Bank too often** – "10 bank visits. Are you dating the banker?"
- **Click a lot** – "1,000 clicks. Carpal tunnel speedrun any%."
- **Hop worlds** – "New world, same you."
- **Log in** – an honest appraisal of your total level.

Roasts appear as overhead text above the heckler, in the chatbox, or both.
Everything is client-side: **nobody else can see it**, so your dignity is safe
from everyone except yourself.

## Config

- Summon heckler (turn him off and the roasts appear above your own head instead)
- Heckler name
- Delivery (overhead / chatbox / both)
- Cooldown between roasts (deaths and level ups always get through)
- Toggle each trigger, plus thresholds for low HP, AFK time, bank trips and clicks
